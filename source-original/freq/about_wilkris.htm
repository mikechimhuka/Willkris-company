QUESTIONS ABOUT WILKRIS COMPANY

 

Wilkris has been in business since 1972 when it was started as "Electrical Systems Consulting" (ESC). The company name was changed and it was incorporated in 1982.

What does Wilkris Company do?

Wilkris has two divisions, manufacturing, and engineering. Manufacturing builds and assembles products. Engineering designs and builds prototypes, and provides engineering services to the manufacturing division and outside customers. These services include the following:

A. Contract engineering services.
B. Consulting services for level sensing and fluid filling.
C. Service and support for Wilkris level sensing and filling products.
